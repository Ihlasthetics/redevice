# AI usage disclosure

AI assistance was used to review the pre-event prototype, plan the Stellar migration, implement the Soroban contract and browser client, and draft tests and documentation. Human team members define the product, review changes, control wallets, and decide when to deploy or publish. The earlier prototype began on Sui and is preserved in Git history; its records are not Stellar-native evidence.

Local automated checks and the Testnet deployment are recorded in the integration plan. The team signed the deployment and demo transactions with its own Freighter wallet; AI assistance did not access a recovery phrase or private key.
